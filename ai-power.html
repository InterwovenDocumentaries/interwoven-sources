Production & use of material

The heater comparison assumes one kilowatt per heater.

Documents and articles are excerpted, reframed and highlighted. Video is excerpted, reframed and retimed without its original soundtrack; Meta construction footage also uses motion interpolation. Photographs are reframed with slow camera movement. Original source text and embedded branding remain part of the quoted material.

Credits identify the work and its creators. They do not imply endorsement or transfer rights in the original material.
